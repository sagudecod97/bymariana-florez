Shaping Dream (2024)

"Fashion and sustainability are two themes that are intrinsically linked today. It is essential to recognize that the fashion industry has a significant impact on the environment, from the production of raw materials to the manufacturing and distribution process. In this sense, the use of denim as an iconic garment in fashion represents an important point to consider.


Jean is the most contaminating material in this industry, and the main character in this capsule, so it is important to highlight that the production of traditional jeans involves a considerable consumption of water, chemicals, and energy. However, in recent years, many fashion brands have been working to develop more sustainable practices, using recycled materials or employing production processes that are more environmentally friendly."

How it started

"At my 6th semester in the Caracas Design Institute (IDC), the DNA of our personal style and own taste, was pretty much developed, and it was a moment where your most favorite ideas were automatically reflected in any area that was assigned to create. In this case, the principal theme was given by the group of professors, and it was Sustainability. Around it, I started to mature my designs to demonstrate what I really would like to express with fabrics and so, clothes."

My ideas organized before starting to sew

The process

"As commented previously, I was completely decided in what my style to design is. Nature has completely turned into the main source of inspiration. The purity of leafs, flowers, trees, colors and organic shapes are unique. The simplicity that we find in geometry, the daily routine of life, the complexity of some formations, textures, are part of “Moldeando El Denim”.


This capsule includes five looks, inspired in different flowers and leafs. Jean was one of the protagonists, because of its versatility, different blue tones, width and composition, it can be styled from a very casual to formal or elegant look."

Gallery

During this last semester, Clo3D classes turned into one of my favorites. Even though at the beginning it was no easy job to learn digital pattern-making, how textures were applied, the runway of each avatar, choosing the poses we wanted, and rendering the project, etc. It was completely worth it to manage a big percentage of this app.


Nowadays, knowing this type of programs changes the way you can imagine your design before really starting making patterns and sewing. It’s a powerful tool that permits you to plan your ideas, correct them digitally, and then bring them to real life. The realism it gives you makes it one of the best apps for fashion design I’ve ever used.


Aside, how useful it can be, having to work with this capsule turned my experience more “magical” and special since I never imagined how realistic and elegant it resulted...
