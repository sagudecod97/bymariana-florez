Células

Cells (2023)

Three themes encompassing and collaborating with fashion and art are represented in Células: abstraction, craftsmanship, and science. As the primary focus of this collection, abstraction is employed to convey the artist’s ideas not in a literal manner, but rather by proposing a novel reality. It invariably transcends our surroundings, intertwining with our imagination to offer us a fresh perspective. Abstraction has become one of my favored themes, captivated by the amalgamation of hues, the creation of irregular forms, asymmetric patterns, and the stylistic combinations that this concept merges.


In the realm of art and design, manual labor has served as the origin of inspiration that has caused rage in the fashion world. Prominent brands have availed themselves of the talents of artisans from diverse nations with the objective of accentuating those techniques in cultures across the globe. Craftsmanship distinguishes itself by the manual production of pieces of any kind. It requires considerable dedication, time, and concentration. The completion of these projects can extend over years. This characteristic serves to differentiate this mode of production from industrial methods. Such manual process is highly esteemed, as its outcomes are unique, with no two products being precisely alike, thereby enhancing the value of the piece.

As the primary objective, futuristic nature is established as the theme to be reinterpreted. Futurism was an Italian artistic movement that emerged in the early twentieth century. In its inception, artists were captivated and revered movement, aviation, and cinematography in all their forms. Thus, an art form was proposed that would celebrate modernity, capturing dynamism, energy, industry, and, of course, technology.


The celebration of the machine age was a pivotal point they emphasized; they glorified speed and violence, leading them to highlight novel techniques such as repetition and blurring. Additionally, they adopted "lines of force" from the Cubists.


As time progressed, Futurism gradually integrated into the realm of fashion, extending beyond sculptures and paintings. Futuristic designs began to emerge in clothing, conceived with the intention of facilitating adaptation to specific environments and ensuring that the fabrics and materials were aesthetically pleasing within those spaces. Futurism in fashion signified a grand vision, introducing novel fabrics, shapes, cuts, and other elements that reflected audacity, risk, movement, and velocity.
